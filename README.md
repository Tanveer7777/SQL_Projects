# 2.SQL Retail Sales Analysis

## Project Description

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data using SQL to identify key sales trends and customer purchasing behavior. The dataset includes transaction details such as customer demographics, product categories, quantity, pricing and sales timing.

SQL was used to clean the data, explore patterns and answer business-related questions to generate meaningful insights.

---

## Tasks Performed

- Performed data cleaning by identifying and removing records with missing values

- Conducted data exploration to identify total transactions, unique customers and product categories

- Analyzed total revenue generated by each product category

- Identified the month with the highest sales revenue

- Calculated the average revenue generated per transaction

- Compared spending patterns between male and female customers

- Identified the top 10 highest spending customers

- Analyzed sales distribution by hour to determine peak shopping times

- Evaluated sales performance across different time periods (morning, afternoon, evening, night)

- Identified the highest revenue generating day of the week

- Analyzed monthly sales trends to observe seasonality patterns

- Examined product categories with the highest quantity sold

- Segmented customers by age groups to analyze purchasing behavior

- Evaluated price sensitivity across different product categories

- Calculated customer lifetime value based on total revenue per customer

- Estimated average purchase frequency per customer

- Identified top and bottom performers on key metrics like satisfaction, attrition based on criteria

- Used SQL techniques like JOINs, GROUP BY, HAVING, CASE statements for complex analysis 

- Organized and presented findings in a clear format for consumption by leadership
